## Divergences

### #1
- decisions.md says: "The front is the one place both ids are in the same hands … On the return — the same moment the `spa_connection_id` cookie is written — the front records the link in both directions".
- the implementation at `src/genro_asgi_multiworker_spa/spa_app.py:1158-1166` does only the cookie: `settled = reply.info.get("connection_id")` / `if settled is not None and settled != carried:` / `response.set_cookie(SPA_CONNECTION_ID_COOKIE, settled, max_age=CONNECTION_COOKIE_MAX_AGE, path="/", httponly=True, samesite="lax")`. Nothing after that line writes a link, in either direction.
- the docstring says: SILENT. `spa_app.py:1143-1151` describes the cookie rewrite only; `status.md` of the entry states it on its own account: "The separately recorded authenticating connection/core session link is not written by `build_response`".
- the handoff of 2026-08-01 (`temp/design_step1_identita_registri_2026-08-01.md`) says: "**Ponte di login** (senso unico): l'atto di login legacy costruisce l'avatar → `session.attach_avatar(...)` → POI aggiorna la voce connections nel formato legacy. La session non scrive mai nel registro legacy fuori dal login." The declared writer is the legacy login act, not the front on the return; the declared direction is one-way, not both.
- the transcript `2390696f-1c5b-4b9d-aaea-370d6eff0d84.jsonl` of 2026-08-24 (assistant) says, uuid `a37c7df1-3362-4347-8891-0daee3acee1d`: "il punto dove i due id **coesistono in mano allo stesso codice** è il front (`SpaApplication`) sul ritorno … Lì il front può dire al commander «questa connessione = questa sessione», sulla lane che già usa." Confirmed by the user in the turn right after, uuid `e064e51d-3701-4340-bf08-17ca784cf9d0`: "la session del server dovrebbe avere la connection id in modo da avere anche l'info opposta".
