# Console

**Version**: 0.1 · **Last Updated**: 2026-08-22 · **Status**: 🔴 DA REVISIONARE

**The need.** A developer facing a live pool asks questions nobody predicted. Our answer: an eval door — composable expressions instead of a fixed menu — priced honestly: mounting IS the gate.

The pool's debug door, served as MCP tools (`SpaConsoleMcpApplication`):
`eval` of a Python expression in the commander process or in any worker,
reached over the lane the commander already holds. Mounting IS the gate —
it is full eval by construction and must never be mounted in production.

Interactions: orchestration (the lane) · routing (MCP surface).
